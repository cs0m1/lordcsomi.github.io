PRESS TO DECREMENT

There is a button, and a number, and the number is ten. Pressing it down to zero doesn't win — it completes a cycle, rebuilds the button slightly worse than it was, and starts again at ten. Placed 50th of 10,600 entries for Creativity (top 0.5%) and 172nd for Narrative, from 29 ratings. Built with no engine and no asset files: native ES modules, everything drawn to a single canvas, every sound synthesized at runtime. A kernel owns the count and knows nothing about any individual cycle, so all the difficulty lives in the cycles and an unfinished one degrades to a plain ten-press round instead of breaking the game.
